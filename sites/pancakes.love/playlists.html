Pancakes, in song

How should we live together?

Three evolving playlists approach the Pancakes argument from different directions. v1 asks what institutions must protect. v2 asks what all that protection is for. v3 asks what ideals become when people build, inhabit, operate, resist, and transform the systems meant to realize them.

v1

Requirements: what institutions must protect

The first sequence listens from inside institutional failure. Songs about labour, authority, coercive care, exile, escape, stewardship, and survival turn lived injuries into demands: share power, recognize hidden work, protect personhood and place, and leave room for life, home, and bread.

  1. There Is Power in a Union
  2. Labour
  3. Fast Car
  4. Bread

v2

Purpose: what civilization is for

The second sequence refuses to make better systems an end in themselves. Power, markets, technology, and politics matter because ordinary people should be able to live ordinary lives extraordinarily well: stay up late, walk away, dance together, come home, watch the sunrise, and celebrate.

  1. Green Onions
  2. Pata Pata
  3. Jerusalema
  4. Celebration
YouTube playlist Notes

v3

Operation: what systems become

The third sequence enters the gap between ideology and lived reality. Ideas become rules, habits, incentives, environments, and machines; these reshape the people inside them, who adapt, withdraw, resist, and change the system in return. Operation is transformation.

  1. Across the Universe
  2. Dear Prudence
  3. Hurt
  4. Ievan Polkka

One evolving argument

Requirements. Purpose. Operation.

v1 begins with consequences and turns them into demands. v2 names the human flourishing those demands are meant to serve. v3 follows a system after it begins operating, when ideals encounter history, incentives, architecture, and human adaptation. Together they ask not only what should exist, but how to keep it worthy of the people living inside it.
