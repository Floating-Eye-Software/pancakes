Songs about ideals, systems, participation, exhaustion, adaptation, memory, and
the people inside the machinery.

The first Pancakes playlist asked what institutions must protect. The second
asked what civilization is for. v3 follows ideals into operation, where they
become rules, roles, incentives, environments, and machines—and are changed by
the people who inhabit, resist, and remake them.

How can systems change without losing their purpose or becoming unworthy of the
people living inside them?

Listen in order if you can.
