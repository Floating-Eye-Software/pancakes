# Pancakes Playlist v3

The first Pancakes playlist asks what institutions must protect. The second
asks what civilization is for. v3 asks what happens when ideals enter history
and become operating systems.

Ideas become rules, roles, habits, incentives, environments, and machines.
Those arrangements transform the people who live inside them; people adapt,
withdraw, resist, reinterpret, and transform the arrangements in return. The
artists and performances make this dynamic audible, but they are not the
playlist's subject. v3 is about ideology in operation.

This directory contains the current v3 sequence, its liner notes and supporting
histories, the combined index of all three playlists, and the companion story
*The Forty-Seventh Frequency*.
